What is negative slicing ?
Negative slicing mein hum negative indexes (-1, -2, -3 etc.) use karke string ka koi hissa nikalte hain.matlab kaam wahi hai pehly wala bas index ki line - mai chalti hai 

For Example:
String "Python" ke indexes dekho:

|    Character | P | y | t | h | o | n |
               |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
Positive Index | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
Negative Index | -6 | -5 | -4 | -3 | -2 | -1 |
